Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026 • iEmoji Keyboard Team

At iEmoji Keyboard, we prioritize user privacy above all else. This Privacy Policy details our commitment to data protection, local sandboxed execution, and absolute zero data collection.

1. Zero Data Collection

iEmoji Keyboard runs 100% locally on your Android device. All key logs, text predictions, custom dictionaries, and emoji selections remain isolated within your device's local application memory.

  • We do NOT collect, store, or transmit what you type.
  • We do NOT collect passwords, credit card numbers, or personal identifying information.
  • We do NOT track analytics, location data, or device identifiers.

2. No Network Permissions Requested

The iEmoji Keyboard package (iemoji-keyboard.apk) does not request Android internet or network communication permissions. It cannot transmit data to remote servers or third-party cloud services.

3. Local Storage Usage

The web-based emoji copy tool and interactive demo on this website use standard web localStorage solely to save your recently copied emojis and favorited emoji preferences locally inside your browser. No data is sent over the network.

4. Landing Page Analytics (Conversion Tracking)

To measure general interest, our website uses a lightweight, privacy-first analytics script (GoatCounter) solely to count aggregate clicks on the "Download APK" buttons. GoatCounter does not use tracking cookies, does not capture IP addresses, and does not collect personal identifiers. This analytics script operates strictly on our website pages and is completely independent of the Android app itself (which contains zero telemetry or trackers).

5. Open Source Transparency

Our application code and web assets are transparently hosted and publicly accessible. Users can audit source files to verify our security claims on GitHub: https://github.com/Daksh-2102/IEmoji.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to our team at dakshvasu21@gmail.com.
